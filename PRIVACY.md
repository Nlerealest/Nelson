# Ndalama privacy policy

_Last updated: 17 October 2026_

Ndalama is a personal money manager. This policy explains what happens to the information you enter.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** Ndalama has no accounts, no servers and no analytics. The income, expenses, budgets, savings goals, bills and loans you record are stored only on your own phone.

## Where your data goes

- Your data never leaves your device unless **you** choose to save a backup file. When you do, you decide where it goes (for example WhatsApp, Google Drive or email), and that service's own privacy policy applies.
- Bill and loan reminders are scheduled on your phone itself. No information is sent anywhere to deliver them.
- Uninstalling the app or using **Settings → Erase all data** permanently deletes everything stored by Ndalama on the phone.

## Permissions

- **Notifications**: optional, used only to remind you about bills and loan repayments.

## Children

Ndalama is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.
