Rhythm: about 83 BPM (0.72 s per beat). The long hold is ~9 beats (6.7 s). Whips take 1.3, 0.7 and 0.6 beats and land on holds of 0.8, 0.7 and (end) 2.3 beats. So: long calm, then an accelerating burst of three whips, each shorter than the last, each landing on a held shot. Every whip has easing-out (a fast start, brief overshoot-free stop), no bounce.

## What makes it work
- **Contrast of tempo:** a very long, still opening, then three hits in 3 seconds. The stillness makes the whips feel fast.
- **Motion blur sells speed**: the blur is horizontal and total during the swing; the held shots are perfectly sharp.
- **The same direction twice** (whips 2 and 3 both pan right) builds momentum; the first whip is the opposite way, so the burst reads as "there, and back".
- **No text, no music, no cuts**: nothing but camera. Each whip hides the join to the next shot.
- Pace of idea changes: 1 per ~0.5-1 s in the burst, 1 per ~7 s in the opening.
