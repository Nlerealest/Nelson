## Full description

> **Ndalama helps you see where your money goes, and how much you can safely spend each day.**
>
> Payday comes, then rent, groceries, transport, money sent home, airtime… and by month end it's gone. Ndalama puts all of it in one place so you stay in control.
>
> **SAFE TO SPEND TODAY**
> Every day, Ndalama shows how much you can spend, after your unpaid bills, debts and savings are set aside.
>
> **RECORD MONEY IN SECONDS**
> Add income and expenses with categories made for life in Malawi: rent, groceries, transport, ESCOM, water, airtime and data, family support, school fees, church and more. Note how you paid: cash, Airtel Money, TNM Mpamba or bank.
>
> **A BUDGET FROM WHAT YOU REALLY EARN**
> Ndalama suggests a monthly budget from your actual income, and warns you when spending is running too fast.
>
> **SAVINGS GOALS WITH EARLY WARNINGS**
> Save for a phone, school fees, a car or a house. See how much to put aside each month, and get warned early if your spending puts a goal at risk.
>
> **BILL REMINDERS**
> Get a reminder the day before and the day a bill is due.
>
> **LOANS AND DEBTS**
> Keep track of money you lend to friends and family, and money you owe, with repayments and due dates.
>
> **MONTHLY REPORTS**
> See exactly where your money went, compared with your budget and with last month.
>
> **PRIVATE AND OFFLINE**
> No account, no internet needed. Your data stays only on your phone. Save a backup to WhatsApp, Google Drive or email whenever you like.

## Permissions and why they're needed

| Permission | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Notifications | Bill and loan repayment reminders (optional; asked only when you turn reminders on) |
| Run at startup | Re-schedules reminders after the phone restarts |
| Vibrate | Lets reminders vibrate |
| Internet | Included by the app framework. Ndalama has no account and sends none of your data anywhere. |

Ndalama does not use contacts, location, camera, microphone or file storage permissions.
